# Delete your SYNC account

**SYNC** by **Lev Albrant**

You can delete your account and its data at any time, with or without the app.

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open SYNC and go to **Settings → Account**.
2. Tap **Delete account**.
3. Type **DELETE**, tap **Delete permanently**, and confirm your password.

It takes effect at once. If you're in a match, finish or forfeit it first.

## Without the app

Email **levalbrant@gmail.com** from your account's address, with the subject
**"Delete my SYNC account"**. We may ask you to confirm from that address. We delete it
within **30 days** and reply when it's done.

## What is deleted

- your sign-in (email and password);
- your callsign, rating, stats and leaderboard entry;
- your current match and matchmaking entry, and any waiting private game you created;
- your profile and leaderboard entry in the SYNC web game, which shares the account.

## What is kept

- **Past matches:** your ID and callsign are replaced with "deleted". Your opponent
  keeps the result, so their history and rating stay correct, but nothing links it to
  you. When both players have deleted their accounts, the match is removed.
- **Past matches in the SYNC web game:** the same. They keep the result, not your name.
- **Server logs** (which may hold your IP address and player ID), kept by Google for
  30 days, then deleted.

Nothing else is kept.
